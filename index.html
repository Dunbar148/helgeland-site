The Seaweed Society

Founded to get the world eating kelp.

Helgeland Seaweed Society is growers and neighbours on the Helgeland coast, with our own small ocean farm at Rødøy, working with the chefs and scientists of Nofima, Norway's national food research institute.

We believe the most sustainable food on Earth grows in the sea, and that people will eat it when it tastes this good. Farmland is stretched thin and the seas are overfished; kelp asks for neither. That is the whole cause. The tins are how we argue it.

Grown on ropes, through the cold months.

Winged kelp, Alaria esculenta, grown on ropes in the open sea at Bukkøya off Rødøy. It is farmed, not wild-harvested: the seabed is left alone, and every batch is traceable to the line it grew on.

Nothing needed, nothing taken.

No farmland, no fresh water, no fertiliser, no pesticides. Kelp draws everything it needs from the sea, and leaves the water cleaner than it found it.

Cooked and canned in Lofoten.

The honest old technology of the can keeps real food real for years. No cold chain, no preservatives. One value chain, one owner, since 2019.

Seaweed Pesto

Nordic umami in a spoon.

99 kr · 120 g · 8 servings

Winged kelp with pumpkin and sunflower seeds, garlic and lemon. Rich, deeply savoury: the kelp does the umami work parmesan usually does. Perfect for pasta, fish and bread.

Request samples

In the kitchen

  • Spread thick on sourdough or dark rye
  • Toss through pasta, gnocchi or roasted vegetables
  • Whip into aioli or beurre blanc for fish and shellfish
  • Fold into dressings and cold sauces

Ingredients

Winged kelp (Alaria esculenta) 33%, pumpkin seeds, sunflower seeds, yeast extract, garlic, sunflower oil 46%, salt, lemon juice, sugar, ground black pepper, chlorophyllin (E141) 0.01%.

No nuts, gluten, dairy, soy or sesame.
